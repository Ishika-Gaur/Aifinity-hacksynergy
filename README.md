# Cognify AI 🧠

> **AI That Turns Struggles into Strengths**

Cognify AI is an intelligent **learning-gap and career-readiness platform** designed to help students understand *why* they make mistakes, identify their conceptual weaknesses, and build a personalized path toward their career goals.

Instead of simply showing students their scores or correct answers, Cognify AI analyzes their learning patterns and transforms mistakes into **personalized learning insights and career-ready actions**.

**Discover → Analyze → Improve → Prepare**

---

## 🚀 Overview

Students often know **what they got wrong**, but not:

* Which concept they are missing
* Why they repeatedly make similar mistakes
* Which prerequisite concepts they need to strengthen
* Whether their current skills match their desired career
* What they should learn next

Cognify AI addresses these problems through three core AI-driven modules:

### 🧠 ConceptRoot AI

Identifies the root cause behind incorrect answers and detects missing prerequisite concepts.

### 📊 MistakeMap AI

Analyzes multiple attempts to discover recurring mistake patterns, learning weaknesses, and performance issues.

### 🎯 SkillGap AI

Connects a student's learning performance and career goals with required skills to generate a personalized career-readiness roadmap.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🧠 ConceptRoot AI

* Root-cause analysis of incorrect answers
* Concept-gap detection
* Identification of missing prerequisite concepts
* Targeted concept recommendations
* Personalized practice guidance
* Adaptive learning paths

### 📊 MistakeMap AI

* Maintains a history of quizzes, coding attempts, and assessments
* Detects recurring mistakes across multiple attempts
* Identifies learning patterns that may not be visible from a single submission
* Classifies mistakes into areas such as:

  * Knowledge gaps
  * Speed issues
  * Accuracy problems
* Provides performance insights
* Generates personalized learning roadmaps

### 🎯 SkillGap AI

SkillGap AI analyzes a student's:

* Learning progress
* Quiz performance
* Resume and projects
* Target career goal

It then identifies missing skills by comparing the student's current profile with career requirements and generates an actionable roadmap.
